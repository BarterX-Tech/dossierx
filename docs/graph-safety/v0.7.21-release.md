## Preserved semantics

These points were verified by source reading and by the proofs below.

- Readiness uses the same algorithm as v0.7.20: SCC, BFS witnesses, global
  `len(prefix+cycle)` minimisation, and `localSummary` as the single cause
  source. The only edge-source change is `RestsOn` becoming `RestsOn.IDs`.
  `Compute` never mutates its inputs (`TestComputeByteForByteInputImmutability`).
- Local approval, dependency readiness and integrated proof stay separate. A
  locked claim on a draft parent is `local_approved` and not `dependency_ready`.
- Selective clearing, diamond baselines and propagation through unchanged
  intermediates behave as before. Cross-version differential: identical
  identities and witnesses.
- Singleton and batch locking both use `EvaluateSetWithSemanticConflicts`.
  Refused sets write nothing (`cmd/dossierx` lock and policy subsets).
- The retired policy-0 store loads as v1. Reads do not write. The next write
  adds only the requested claim's records plus `policy_version`,
  `policy_migrated_at` and `policy_migration_reason`. No approval, baseline,
  receipt or cause changes (proof B). **This holds only for stores the candidate
  can parse (see G1).**

## Approved changes and where they are documented

All of these are in `CHANGELOG.md` under `[0.7.21]`, Changed and Removed:

- Policy 0 is retired and carries over to v1. Readiness reports `policy_version: 1`
  for every store.
- `governed_by`, `mirrors`, `build_role`, the doctrine hub and hub gating,
  `rest-on-locked`, `dependency_not_locked` and build order are removed.
  `BaselineDependencyIDs == rests_on`. Every `ContentHash` and
  `LockedClaimHash` moves, and every locked claim re-locks once by hand
  (upgrade steps 8-9).
- `rests_on` is required (list or `{none, reason}`), its targets are
  restricted, and the mapping is strict.
- `catalog.json` is an integration projection. Internals claims and edges are
  omitted, internals ids in readiness become `(internals)`, and
  `edges.rests_on_internals_omitted` is counted. See
  [v0.7.21-catalog-internals-redaction.md](v0.7.21-catalog-internals-redaction.md).
- The graph payload is schema 3 (`build_role` removed). The manifest,
  constitution roof gate, caps and isolation-view refusal are new `check` and
  lock gates.

Known oracle exceptions: none were needed. The v0.7.20 engine and the
candidate agree exactly on every generated case once the documented
`policy_version` label is mapped.

## Worst-case bounds

These bounds are unchanged from v0.7.20 because the algorithm is unchanged.

- One query: O(V + E) for traversal plus a BFS per cyclic SCC entry, with no
  path enumeration.
- All claims: records R ≤ V·F_reachable, where each record carries at most one
  witness of length ≤ V. Catalog bytes are O(V·(V+E_b)·D) on deep or dense
  graphs, as the skill states.
- Catalog redaction is a single O(R·D) pass over the emitted records (see the
  redaction record).
- Budgets (asserted in tests):
  - `TestAuditScale`: fact bound, compute budget, output-byte budget.
  - `TestConformanceEndToEndGraphScaleBounds`: 2 s wall time (non-race), 512 MiB
    TotalAlloc, and `conformance.MaxOutputBytes` per file.
  - Skill containment: catalog.json below 64 MB.

## Defects

**G1: a lock store written by v0.7.20 cannot be read by the candidate.**

- Where:
  - `internal/model/rests_on.go:37`: `RestsOn` became a struct with only
    `UnmarshalYAML` (`:81`) and no JSON decoder.
  - `internal/lock/ledger.go:150`: `LedgerRecord.Content *model.Claim`.
  - `internal/lock/lock.go:112`: `DependencyReceipt.Content model.Claim`.
  - These are decoded at `internal/lock/lock.go:426` (ledger) and `:489`
    (receipts).
- Cause: v0.7.20 serialized the approved claim's `RestsOn` as a JSON array
  (`"RestsOn": ["widget.contract.a"]`). The candidate expects
  `{"None":…,"Reason":…,"IDs":[…]}`. So `LoadStore` fails with
  `json: cannot unmarshal array into Go struct field Claim.content.RestsOn of type model.RestsOn`.
- Scope: any v0.7.20 project, policy 0 or v1, whose ledger holds an approved
  claim with at least one `rests_on` target. `repro_min.sh` reproduces it with
  an untouched v0.7.20 v1 store holding two claims (a, and b rests_on a).
- What a consumer observes after upgrading and folding the corpus as
  `CHANGELOG.md` instructs:
  - `check --validate` fails `integrity_failed` with `lock-ledger-unreadable`
    plus one `lock-ledger-missing` per locked claim. Its hint says to restore
    the ledger from version control and "Do NOT re-lock", but the file is
    intact, so restoring it changes nothing.
  - `claim show` reports every claim `local_approved: false` with
    `approval_unknown`, including claims that themselves rest on nothing. It
    fails closed, so nothing looks ready, but every approval is lost from
    view.
  - `constitution lock` (upgrade step 4), `claim unlock`, and `claim lock
    --dry-run` (step 9) exit `write_failed` with the parse error above.
  - The documented v0.7.20 → v0.7.21 upgrade cannot proceed without
    hand-editing `build/ledger/lock-store.json`.
- Coverage gap: no committed test loads a ledger or receipt written by
  v0.7.20. `TestRetiredPolicy0StoreReadsAsV1AndStampsOnWrite` uses a store with
  no approvals.
- Not fixed here, as instructed.

No other defect was demonstrated.
