## The surface: roadmap

ROADMAP.md is the deferred and unverified register, and it sets expectations for
anyone deciding to depend on this project.

FALSIFICATION IS INVERTED HERE. The question is not whether an entry promises
something that has not arrived — a roadmap is allowed to be unfinished. The
question is whether THIS RELEASE SHIPPED something an entry still calls deferred,
unverified or planned. An entry describing as future work a command, flag or
behaviour that now exists in the inventory is false, and it is false in the
direction that makes a reader underestimate what they already have.

Check every entry against the inventory and the delta in that direction, and
report each one that has been overtaken.
